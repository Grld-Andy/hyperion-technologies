One of the most common questions we hear from new students is simple: where do I start?

## If you're brand new to IT

CompTIA A+ is designed exactly for this. It covers hardware, operating systems, and general troubleshooting — the foundation every technician needs regardless of specialization.

## If you already know you want networking

Cisco CCNA is a strong direct entry point. It's more focused than A+, and it opens the door to the entire Cisco certification track (CCNP, CCIE) if you decide to specialize further.

There's no universally "correct" answer — it depends on your background and goals. Book a conversation with our team and we'll help you map a path that fits.
