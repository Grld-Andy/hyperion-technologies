Reading about VLANs and routing protocols only gets you so far. Here are five habits we consistently see in students who do well in the lab.

1. **Predict before you configure.** Before typing a command, guess what should happen.
2. **Break things on purpose.** Understanding failure modes builds real troubleshooting instinct.
3. **Narrate your own commands.** Say out loud what each line does — gaps in your explanation reveal gaps in understanding.
4. **Keep a running notebook of commands**, organized by task, not by memorized order.
5. **Repeat labs from memory** a few days later, without notes, to check retention.

Consistent, deliberate lab practice beats long, unfocused study sessions every time.
